answered

How fast is the turnaround?
Standard dry cleaning is typically ready within 24–48 hours. Same-day service is available on request for urgent items — just let us know when you book.
Do you really pick up and deliver?
Yes — free doorstep pickup and delivery across Rajajinagar and nearby areas. Book by phone or the form on this page and we'll arrange a convenient slot.
Can you handle designer & wedding garments?
Absolutely. Lehengas, sherwanis, silk sarees and gowns are hand-finished with extra care, careful spotting of embroidery and zari, and safe pressing. High-value pieces are quoted on assessment.
What about tough stains?
Every garment is inspected and spot-treated before cleaning. We'll always try our best on stains — and we'll tell you honestly if something can't be fully removed rather than risk the fabric.
How do I pay?
We accept UPI and cash. Payment is collected on delivery or at the store — whatever is easiest for you.
